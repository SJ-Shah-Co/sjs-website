What we do

A studio and holding company for products we believe in.

Rather than chase a single market, we incubate and operate a focused portfolio across software, applied AI, security, data, and consumer media. Each property is run as its own product with its own users, while shared infrastructure and expertise keep them efficient.

Software & AI

We design, build, and ship production software — including applied AI and developer-focused tools — under our own brands.

Security & Data

Cyber intelligence and data-science properties that turn raw signals into usable insight for technical audiences.

Media & Consumer

Consumer-facing media and lifestyle brands that experiment with new formats and audiences.

IT Consulting

Through SOHUM.agency, we take on occasional consulting engagements — advising teams on architecture, delivery, and modern tooling.

Long-term operation

We hold and operate our properties for the long run, reinvesting in the ones that earn their keep.

Independent & funded

Privately owned and self-directed, so we can build on our own timeline and standards.
