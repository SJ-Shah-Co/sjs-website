Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SJ Shah & Co LLC ("SJ Shah & Co," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with this website, sjshahco.com. Individual products and properties we operate may have their own privacy policies that govern their own services.

Who we are

SJ Shah & Co LLC is a Texas limited liability company that develops and operates technology and media properties and provides business and technology consulting services (through its SOHUM.agency trade name). You can reach us at hello@sjshahco.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you contact us — for example by using the contact form (which opens your own email application) or by emailing us directly — we receive your name, email address, and the contents of your message.
  • Basic technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, for security and to keep the site running.

This website does not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell personal information.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide the information or services you request.
  • To operate, maintain, and secure this website.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only:

  • With service providers who help us operate this website (for example, our hosting provider), acting on our behalf;
  • If required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request; or
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of SJ Shah & Co, our users, or others.

Cookies

This website is a static informational site and does not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Any cookies used by our hosting provider are limited to those necessary to serve and secure the site.

Data retention

We keep correspondence and related information only for as long as needed to respond to you, to maintain our business records, or as required by law.

Your choices

You may contact us at hello@sjshahco.com to ask what information we hold about you, to request corrections, or to request deletion, subject to any legal obligations that require us to retain it.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email hello@sjshahco.com.
